# Retained grants

SHABBAT-LEGACY-MIT.txt retains the original WizardLoop MIT permission for historic source/contributions. The new application grant is the full root LICENSE (AGPL-3.0-only), not an alternative blanket MIT grant.

CC-BY-4.0.txt and CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt are the unchanged plain legal texts downloaded on 2026-10-13 from the official Creative Commons endpoints:

- https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.txt
- https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.txt

Their resource scope, attribution, pinned sources and modifications are described in [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) and the bundled resource metadata. Dependency grants remain in their installed source, included in the corresponding-source archive and indexed by [dependency evidence](../docs/DEPENDENCIES.md).

The unmodified igbinary 3.2.16 extension retains its original [BSD-3-Clause COPYING](../resources/release/preferred-source/additional-source/igbinary/COPYING) beside the pinned source archive. Its notices are preserved rather than replaced with the application grant.
